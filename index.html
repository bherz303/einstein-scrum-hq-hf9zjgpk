SCRUM Certification System

A live scoring, tracking, and training system for the 5 CTXH branch Monday boards — turns board hygiene from a gut call into a graded, trend-tracked, coachable standard. Covers the 12-criterion rubric, a manager-facing reference sheet, a live performance dashboard with full scorecard detail per read, and a hands-on training board with automations that mirror the real branch boards. "Done" looks like: every CTXH manager can self-check their board against a public rubric, Brian can grade any branch in minutes with a defensible score, and new managers certify on a mock board before ever touching a live one.

Where it stands today

Rubric v1.5 shipped — one bar, twelve equal criteria, and a weekly ladder.

Rubric v1.5 landed 8/26/2026 — the largest structural change since the system was built, triggered by a seven-point discrepancy review from Mike's region. Twelve criteria at 5 points each, 60 total, and nothing scores 0; the old "bonus" pair is gone, replaced by two equally-weighted sections — Fundamentals (10) and Conscientious Details (2). The certification line is stated once and stated everywhere: 85%, or 51 of 60. Individuals are now scored on all twelve criteria out of 60 rather than six out of 30, closing a loophole where a manager could post a clean 30/30 while dragging the branch on the three criteria that weren't on their sheet. Maintenance moves to a weekly live-board read with a rolling-window ladder. 31 reads and 2 certifications are logged; all of them predate v1.5 and are marked testing — kept for history, excluded from ladder math. Rippling delivery module and the BM live-walkthrough checklist are still open.

8/26/2026 · later same day
First two certifications re-scored under v1.5 — and a v1.5 bug caught in the process. Brian's call was to re-run Calvin and Joel before grading the remaining 12, so the whole cohort sits on one rubric. Doing it surfaced a defect in the rubric that had shipped hours earlier.

The bug. v1.5 changed Definition of Done to a movement-derived population — grade only what moved into Done since the previous read — to stop bulk cleanup sweeps being graded on a live board. But the mock training board's planted C10 error is "3 of the 4 Done items have no completion note," and those items are dated July by design. A movement window excludes them, so a trainee could execute the taught fix perfectly and still score 1. The planted error became unfixable, and the practical ceiling on a mock board dropped to 55.

The fix (Brian's option B). A mock certification read grades the Done group as planted — all items, any date, damage claims still excluded. The movement window applies only to live weekly reads, which is what it was built for. Max stays 60, nothing is exempted, and all twelve criteria stay testable. Two alternatives were rejected: exempting C10 on mock reads (loses a criterion and leaves the planted error untestable) and re-dating the planted Done items across all 15 mock boards (15 board edits, and it would need redoing every cohort).

Calvin Hughes — 52 of 60, 87%, B, certified. Unchanged total from v1.4, different composition. 8/26/2026
Rubric v1.5 — reconciled against Mike's discrepancy review. Mike's region sent seven findings on how the board is graded. Five were confirmed against the live files, one turned out to be on DFWT's side, and one was half-right. Three more surfaced during the audit that nobody had flagged.

Confirmed and fixed: (1) "Bonus" was misleading — the two bonus criteria were a sixth of the score and the harshest, scoring 0 on a red where regular criteria floored at 1, so the two items a skimmer reads as optional cost the most to miss. The asymmetry is gone; all twelve criteria now floor at 1, and the sections are renamed Fundamentals and Conscientious Details. (2) Updates with Context graded a different list in its green row than its yellow row — sales appeared in one and not the other, so two graders could score the same board differently. One list now, used in every row. (3) No N/A rules existed for empty scope; resolved by ruling that the max is always 60 — empty scope is a confirmed pass on the negative criteria and a miss on the positive ones. (4) B was labeled "Strong" while two consecutive B's pulled certification; the bar and the trigger are now the same number. (5) Definition of Done asked for a column and a posted update interchangeably; it now accepts both with a hierarchy — a real update is 5, a DoD that genuinely covers the title is 3, neither is 1.

Sent back: the daily-LOW target. All six of Brian's files say 15–20, matching Mike's sheet — the 15–25 figure lives on DFWT's side. And the Happiness cadence was already in the training module the whole time; v1.5 moves it into the graded criterion where it belongs.

Found during the audit, not in the review: (1) Criterion 4 measured different things in the grader than in the published sheet — the grader checked QG Value usage, the sheet checked QG pulse visibility. Split: criterion 4 is Happiness only, QG visibility folded into criterion 5. (2) Full Eval advertises 100 points but lists seven Meeting Management items, which totals 95. No Full Eval has ever been run, so nothing behind us is wrong — still open, awaiting Brian's call on whether an eighth item was dropped or the max should read 95. (3) The pass bar appeared only in the rollout announcement and was absent from the reference sheet managers self-check against. Now printed on the sheet.

Brian's rulings. Certification line 85% (51/60) — Calvin's 87% stands. Recovery is 85%+ on two consecutive live reads. One miss is not a pattern — it logs a redirect for the team, nothing more; recertification triggers on three misses inside any rolling four reads, or two consecutive reads at 44 or below. Definition of Done moved into Fundamentals, Project Column into Conscientious Details. Individuals scored on all twelve criteria out of 60, because an individual's pulses are the board. BMs clear two bars — their own score and their board's. Mock certifies, live maintains, weekly. Empty Later group and no QG pulses owned are both misses.

Measurement work behind the changes. Confirmed the scored surface is fully readable (50–161 active items per board) and killed the sampling licence that let a 5-point criterion rest on a ~7% read of South Austin's 140 active pulses. Found that windowing Definition of Done on updated_at would have been wrong: South Austin had 34 Done items sharing a timestamp to the second and 40 more in a single burst the day before — cleanup sweeps, not completions. The population is now derived from what actually moved into Done since the previous read.

Data migration. Criterion keys migrated from BH-*/BONUS-* to C1–C12 with the old mapping preserved in meta.criteria_map — keys that contradicted their section were exactly the drift that started this review. All 31 evals and both certifications tagged phase: "testing"; nothing deleted. The uniform 1-point floor moved two logged scores (South Austin 7/21 → 44, Leander 7/21 → 56); no grade changed. Two pre-existing arithmetic errors surfaced and were deliberately left alone — the 7/14 North Austin and South Austin reads have stored scores that don't equal the sum of their own criteria (55 vs 52, and 50 vs 52). Both are flagged in the data with a data_note rather than silently corrected; they are testing-phase entries, so nothing live depends on them, but Brian should pick the right number.
